## Volunteer Availability System

*A web-based volunteer availability management system designed to simplify how an organization collects, organizes, and communicates volunteer availability.*

**The Problem**

The organization needed a simpler way to collect and manage volunteer availability. The goal was to make it easier for volunteers to communicate their schedules while giving administrators a centralized view of that information.

**The Solution**

I developed a web application that allowed volunteers to manage their availability through a calendar while providing administrators with tools to review availability and support scheduling.

The system included authentication, role-based access, email communication, and reporting.

**Key Features**
>Calendar — Volunteers can view and manage their availability through a calendar interface.

>Email — Automated email communication supports scheduling and volunteer coordination.

>Reports — Administrators can review and organize availability information for planning.

>Authentication & Roles — Different access levels provide volunteers and administrators with appropriate functionality.

>Admin View — Centralized access to volunteer availability.

>Design & Development Process

**The initial solution was built as a web application using JavaScript, Supabase, and FullCalendar.**

**After receiving stakeholder feedback, I reassessed the workflow and redesigned the solution around Google Forms and Google Apps Script.**

This iteration demonstrated an important part of the development process: the best technical solution isn't always the most complex one. The revised workflow simplified data collection while still supporting the organization's scheduling needs.

*Project Background*

This project became the foundation for my Assembly Hall Admin Console, which builds on the availability data and explores the next question: **how can individual volunteer availability be transformed into organizational insight?**

**While Volunteer Availability focuses on collecting and communicating availability, the Admin Console focuses on understanding coverage and supporting data-driven decisions.**
